The Punishment

For whoever finishes dead last, the Toilet Bowl champion of losing

🛒 THE WALMART GAUNTLET

Official punishment terms — binding and non-negotiable

The loser must visit 14 different Walmart locations within a single 30-day period. The loser chooses which calendar month to complete the gauntlet.

At each of the 14 Walmarts, the loser must purchase one item beginning with the first letter of each league member's name. (Alkesh in the league? Better buy an apple.)

  1. 14 unique locations. No Walmart may be visited twice.
  2. Receipts required for every single purchase.
  3. Photo proof. Loser must be photographed standing in front of the Walmart, holding the item, with the receipt clearly visible.
  4. Time limit. All 14 visits and purchases must be completed inside the selected 30-day month.
  5. Selection window. The loser must choose their punishment month within 21 days of losing the championship. Once chosen, that's the month — no swapping later.

Failure to complete the punishment as outlined results in whatever additional league-sanctioned humiliation the commissioners deem appropriate — up to and including relegation or removal from the league. We hope it doesn't come to that. We also kind of hope it does, for content purposes.
